One image that sums up the world's news of Saturday 21 November 2026:
52 stories from 7 regions — Rutte flies to Warsaw as NATO answers
Poland, Zelensky calls Putin a "killer", Saudi air defences down three
Houthi missiles at Riyadh, the IRGC claims a US drone over Hormuz, and
more, with a DATE CHECK box naming the big stories left out because
they broke on another day. Entry for the [make.fun](https://make.fun/c/news-image-20261121)
competition "Sum up 21 November's world news in one image".

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) writes `entry.png` to the
output directory. Run it with `mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py`.
